Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information you share with Navo Builders responsibly.

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Navo Builders (“Navo Builders,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, protects, and handles personal information provided through our website and project inquiry forms.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, property or project details, and other information you submit when requesting an estimate or contacting us. Our website may also collect limited technical information, such as browser or device information, to support website operation and security.

How We Collect Information

We collect information when you complete a form, request an estimate, contact us by phone or email, or otherwise interact with features of our website that ask for information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To review and respond to project inquiries and estimate requests.
  • To communicate with you about your project, appointment, or requested service.
  • To provide, improve, and maintain our website and services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, security, or administrative requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may use trusted service providers when needed to operate our website, communications, scheduling, or business processes. Such providers may access information only as necessary for the services they provide. We do not share text-messaging opt-in or consent information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, performance, security, or understanding website usage. You can manage available cookie controls through your browser settings.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or ask about how your information is being used. Contact us using the information below to make a request.

Children’s Information

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised.
