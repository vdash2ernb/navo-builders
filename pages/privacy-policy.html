Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Navo Builders may collect, use, store, and share information when you visit our website, request an estimate, contact our team, or interact with our online services.

Last updated: July 29, 2026. Navo Builders uses information submitted through the website primarily to respond to inquiries, evaluate projects, prepare estimates, coordinate services, improve the website, and protect our business and visitors.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Navo Builders website, online estimate and contact forms, phone communications initiated through the website, and related online interactions.

This policy does not control the practices of third-party websites, platforms, payment providers, analytics services, social networks, or other services that may be linked from our website.

2. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

  • Name, phone number, email address, and preferred contact details.
  • Property location, project type, requested services, budget or timing information, and project descriptions.
  • Photographs, plans, drawings, measurements, permits, or other files you choose to provide.
  • Messages, feedback, appointment details, and other communications with Navo Builders.

Information collected automatically

  • Internet Protocol address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from technical information.
  • Website interaction data, such as dates and times of visits, links selected, and form or navigation activity.
  • Cookie, analytics, hosting, security, and performance information when those technologies are enabled.

3. How We May Use Information

  • Respond to questions, estimate requests, and service inquiries.
  • Evaluate project feasibility and communicate about consultations, designs, proposals, scheduling, permits, construction, warranties, or follow-up services.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records, prevent fraud or misuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Send service-related updates or marketing communications where permitted. You may ask us to stop promotional messages at any time.

4. How Information May Be Shared

Navo Builders may share information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Service providers supporting website hosting, communications, scheduling, customer relationship management, document storage, analytics, security, estimating, or business operations.
  • Architects, designers, engineers, subcontractors, suppliers, permitting professionals, utilities, inspectors, or other project participants when necessary for a requested service.
  • Government authorities, legal advisors, insurers, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, property, or the integrity of our services.
  • A successor or involved party in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, asset transfer, or similar business transaction.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain analytics or advertising technologies may be treated as “sharing” under some privacy laws when they are enabled.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, server logs, or similar technologies to remember preferences, support essential functionality, measure traffic, understand site performance, prevent abuse, and improve marketing.

You can adjust cookie controls through your browser. Blocking some technologies may affect website functionality. Third-party services may maintain their own privacy and opt-out tools.

6. Data Retention and Security

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain project and business records, resolve disputes, meet contractual requirements, and comply with legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information by contacting us. You may also ask questions about how information has been used or shared.

Depending on where you live and whether a particular privacy law applies, you may have additional rights, such as the right to know, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You may opt out of promotional communications by following the instructions in the message or contacting us. Service, transaction, safety, or project-related messages may still be sent when necessary.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website and construction services are intended for adults and property decision-makers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information may contact us to request review or deletion.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, vendors, business practices, or legal obligations. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

10. Contact Navo Builders

For privacy questions or requests, contact Navo Builders through the website contact form, by phone, or by mail.
